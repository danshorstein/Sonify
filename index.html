Sonification Grammar Lab

Explore how data encodings from visualization can translate into sound: pitch, time, rhythm, chords, motifs, timbre, and ambient state cues.

Core idea

Visualizations encode data with x, y, color, size, shape, and marks. Sonify experiments with equivalent audio encodings: time, pitch, timbre, harmony, rhythm, articulation, and voice.

Rule of thumb Encode based on perceptual fit, not novelty.

Import a Vega-Lite or Altair chart

Paste Vega-Lite JSON with inline data.values. From Altair, that is simply chart.to_dict(). Supported marks: bar, line (single or multi-series), and point/circle. Sonify suggests audio mappings (x→time, y→pitch, color→timbre+pan, size→loudness) that you can override above.
